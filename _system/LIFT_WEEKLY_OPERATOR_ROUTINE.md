# Lift Weekly Operator Routine

2026-07-06. The weekly rhythm that runs Lift Studio without it consuming Megan's week. Three versions by available time; pick the one that fits, don't force the 10-hour version in a 2-hour week.

## Weekly time blocks (the 5-hour default)

| Block | Day | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prospecting | Monday | 1 hr | Source and score 8–10 new candidates (Tier A/B first); add qualifying ones (13+ on the rubric) to the tracker. |
| Audit batch | Tue/Wed | 2 hr | 3–4 audit-lite passes on new prospects; full `$liftaudit` depth on any prior replies. |
| Visual / outreach | Thursday | 1 hr | Produce any needed visuals (ChatGPT); draft and send the week's first-touch batch plus due Follow-Up 1/2, each through the Anti-AI Output QA pass first. |
| Follow-up + tracker review | Friday | 45 min | Log replies, update pipeline stages, queue next week's follow-ups, confirm same-session tracker discipline held all week. |
| Learning note | Friday | 15 min | One or two lines: what got a reply, what didn't, what to adjust — feeds the 90-day plan's learning columns. |

## 2-hour/week version (maintenance mode)

For a busy week where the only goal is keeping the pipeline alive:
- 30 min prospecting (3–5 new candidates).
- 45 min audits (2 audit-lite passes).
- 30 min outreach (send what's ready + one round of due follow-ups).
- 15 min tracker update.
- No dedicated visual block — reuse prior visuals or skip visuals that week rather than rushing one.

## 5-hour/week version (default steady rhythm)

The block table above. This is the target rhythm once past the first two proof-run weeks — enough volume to keep the 90-day plan's numbers moving without turning Lift into a second full-time job.

## 10-hour/week version (paid-delivery / scaling mode)

The 5-hour rhythm above, plus ~5 hours of client fulfillment once paying clients exist:
- 2–3 hrs: write, optimize, and publish blog posts for active Blog Essentials/Growth clients (2 posts/mo at Essentials, 4/mo at Growth — pace accordingly).
- 1 hr: monthly performance notes for active clients.
- 1–2 hrs: content calendar upkeep for active social-lane clients (Starter Content Kit/Content Bank).

Move to this version only once there's actual paid delivery work to do — don't pre-build a 10-hour week's worth of process before the first client exists.

## Division of labor

- **ChatGPT:** runs `$liftaudit` (website/Instagram/SEO-GEO evaluation, visual generation via chat-visual-handoff), drafts outreach and follow-up emails.
- **Claude Code:** prospecting-support scripts/reports (once built per the build queue), tracker validation reports, skill/SOP maintenance, KPI tracker updates, Human Voice QA checklist runs, any doc-only build ticket.
- **Codex:** quarterly no-revival sweep on legacy automation (ticket T10), any future ticket touching Gmail/Sheet-adjacent code design, URL/reference-consistency sweeps.
- **Megan:** every send, every opportunity-selection call, every visual QA sign-off, every price/package decision, every tracker approval, final Anti-AI Output QA acceptance.

## What should wait

Anything gated behind the manual-proof clock — sent-folder reconciliation, the audit-packet-into-Gmail-adjacent flow, any P2/P3 build ticket. A slow week is not a reason to pull these forward; the gate is proof-based, not calendar-based.

## What should not consume Fable/Opus

Routine weekly operation, full stop. Fable/Opus involvement stays reserved for security-boundary design work (the fail-closed web app patch design, the reconciliation write-path gate design) per `LIFT_AGENT_ORCHESTRATION_BLUEPRINT.md` — never for running a normal week.
