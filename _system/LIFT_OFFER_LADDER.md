### Sales angle, proof needed, assets required, agent-generatable, Megan-must-approve — by rung

**0. Free outreach hook** — Angle: one sharp, specific observation earns the right to a reply. Proof needed: response rate by vertical (none yet — day-1 metric of the 90-day plan). Assets: nothing beyond the lite audit itself. Agent-generatable: first draft of the observation + email skeleton. Megan must approve: every send (permanent, no exceptions), and that the observation is actually specific (Anti-AI QA check #2).

**1. Mini-Audit ($250)** — Angle: "see exactly where you stand before spending on anything bigger." This is the flagship because it's already fully scoped, already priced, and nearly matches what the audit skill already produces. **Sharpest open question in this ladder:** the existing `$liftaudit` skill produces a full two-channel deep audit (research, verified asset pack, concept briefs, chat-visual-handoff bundle, outreach email) — meaningfully more than the menu's Mini-Audit scope (website + social review, first-impression notes, 5–7 quick wins, next step). Running full audit depth on every cold prospect for a $250 price point (or before any reply signal) risks spending deep-audit hours on prospects who never respond. Recommend two audit depths going forward — **audit-lite** for cold outreach and the free hook, **full `$liftaudit`** reserved for the paid $250 Mini-Audit itself or any prospect who has already replied — detailed in `_system/LIFT_AUDIT_PRODUCTION_SYSTEM.md`. Proof needed: real Megan-hours per Mini-Audit once a few are sold (currently unmeasured). Assets: live site + social review only. Agent-generatable: research pass, first-impression notes draft. Megan must approve: the two flagged opportunities (taste), final copy (Anti-AI QA), send/delivery.

**2. Blog & SEO Foundation ($600–1,200)** — Angle: "here's the plan the retainer executes." Natural bridge from Mini-Audit finding ("no blog") to a recurring commitment. Proof needed: none yet — first sale of this rung will set the real time cost. Assets: keyword research, ranking snapshot, content pillar map. Agent-generatable: keyword/topic research draft, seasonal calendar draft. Megan must approve: pillar strategy, final plan document (Anti-AI QA — this is a strategy document a client reads closely; generic-sounding pillars undercut the sale).

**3–4. Blog Essentials / Blog Growth ($500–750 / $900–1,400 per month)** — Angle: "social builds your feed, blog gets you found in search" (existing menu positioning line — keep it, it's already sharp and non-generic). This is the **profit core** — recurring revenue is the actual money machine, not one-time audits. Proof needed: real per-post time cost; this is the single most important number to learn in the first 60 days, because it determines whether $500–750/mo is profitable or break-even. Assets: real client project photos, service pages to link internally. Agent-generatable: first-draft post copy, internal-link suggestions, monthly performance note draft. Megan must approve: every post before publish (client-facing, Anti-AI QA mandatory — generic blog copy fails both the client relationship and the actual SEO goal, since generic content doesn't rank either), monthly report tone.

**5–6. Starter Content Kit / Content Bank ($650 / $950 per month)** — Angle: "consistency without a full retainer." Proof needed: none yet; secondary lane to Blog, useful for prospects whose weakness is social rather than search. Assets: real FAQs/objections/services to build concepts from — generic concepts are an Anti-AI QA failure (empty adjectives, no prospect-specific observation) and also just bad content. Agent-generatable: concept drafts, caption hooks, calendar draft. Megan must approve: final concept selection (taste), caption tone.

**7–8. Instagram Refresh / UGC Script Pack ($250 / $300)** — Angle: cheap, fast, single-pass upsell after any other close — "while we're in here." Proof needed: none, low risk either way. Assets: current profile/highlights, brand colors. Agent-generatable: bio draft, highlight structure, script drafts. Megan must approve: final bio copy (Anti-AI QA — bios are exactly where "elevate your brand" language creeps in), script realism.

**9. Brand Foundation ($900–1,800)** — Angle: for prospects whose core problem is "we don't know who we are," not "we're invisible in search." Proof needed: none yet; keep this rung opportunistic, not a volume play. Assets: full discovery conversation, competitor identity scan. Agent-generatable: mood-board-level research only. Megan must approve: everything — this is the most taste-heavy, least templatable rung on the ladder, by design.

## Gap flagged, not a rung yet: "Website Facelift"

The audit skill already produces a website concept brief and a ChatGPT visual-handoff bundle as a **free deliverable inside the audit** — but there is no paid, productized tier for actually *building* that concept into a live site. This is a real decision gap, not an oversight to quietly fix:

- **Option A:** explicitly position Lift as not building/developing websites — the concept brief is a sales asset for the audit, full stop, and prospects who want it built are referred out or declined.
- **Option B:** add a productized "Website Build" tier later, once there's evidence prospects are asking for it after seeing the concept brief.

Recommend **Option A for now** — building sites is a different skill/time commitment than the rest of this ladder and would dilute focus during the 90-day proof window. Revisit only if multiple prospects independently ask for it.
