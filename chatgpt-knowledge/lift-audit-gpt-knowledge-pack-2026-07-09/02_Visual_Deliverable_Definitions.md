# Visual Deliverable Definitions

## Website Facelift

A polished evolution of the real website using:

- exact official logo
- verified brand colors
- official or clearly labeled imagery
- real services and audiences
- accurate calls to action
- improved hierarchy, navigation, spacing, and conversion

It is not:

- a generic rebrand
- a full audit board
- an SEO graphic
- a blog redesign
- an Instagram audit
- an email layout

## Search-to-Sale

Use for:

- retail
- products
- residential real estate
- consumer purchases
- broad lead generation

Path:

High-intent search → helpful content → trust → relevant product/service/property page → inquiry or purchase → revenue

## Search-to-Service

Use for:

- plumbing
- HVAC
- landscaping
- electrical
- healthcare
- professional services
- repair and appointment businesses

Path:

Problem search → useful answer → service/location page → trust signals → call or booking → service revenue

## Search-to-Deal

Use for:

- commercial real estate
- B2B services
- consulting
- long sales cycles
- advisory businesses

Path:

Business search → market insight → proof/listings/case studies → consultation → transaction or engagement

## Search-to-Booking

Use for:

- hospitality
- beauty
- wellness
- restaurants
- appointments
- reservations

Path:

Discovery search → useful content → service/menu/location page → trust → booking

## Instagram Audit

A polished future-state profile or grid concept showing:

- stronger positioning
- clearer content pillars
- better use of official imagery
- local/community content
- educational posts
- social proof
- promotional posts
- conversion pathways

It is not:

- a generic marketing report
- a combined website and social board
- an invented product or lifestyle campaign

## Blog / SEO Opportunity Visual

A strategic conversion-path graphic.

It is not a redesigned blog webpage.

It should show:

- what customers search
- what the business should publish
- how content builds trust
- where the visitor goes next
- how the content supports inquiries, bookings, estimates, sales, or deals
