# SEO, GEO, and Search Strategy Framework

## Core Principle

Do not recommend “more blog posts” without a conversion system.

The goal is:

Search demand → useful content → business proof → relevant page → action → revenue

## Evaluate

- Current ranking visibility
- Local service terms
- High-intent problem searches
- Existing blog consistency
- Topic relevance
- Commercial alignment
- Internal linking
- Service pages
- Location pages
- Google Business Profile alignment
- Calls to action
- AI-search readability
- Content refresh opportunities

## Content Types

- Evergreen search articles
- Seasonal articles
- Service explainers
- Comparison articles
- Cost and planning guides
- Problem/symptom content
- Local guides
- Case studies
- Township/community pages
- FAQ content
- Existing article refreshes

## GEO / AI Discovery Structure

Content should include:

- clear answers
- concise headings
- direct explanations
- local context
- structured questions
- strong internal links
- authoritative proof
- relevant service connections
- clear next steps

## Local SEO Priorities

- service + city pages
- consistent NAP information
- localized title tags and headings
- reviews and proof near CTAs
- local internal links
- Google Business Profile alignment
- service-area clarity
- unique location content

## Search Visual Logic

### Search-to-Service
Problem search → article → local service page → trust → call/booking

### Search-to-Sale
Product or buyer search → guide → product/property/service page → proof → purchase/inquiry

### Search-to-Deal
Commercial search → market insight → listing/service/case study → consultation → deal

### Search-to-Booking
Discovery search → useful content → service/menu/location page → booking

## Blog Audit Questions

- Is publishing consistent?
- Are topics commercially relevant?
- Are categories too broad?
- Are older posts worth refreshing?
- Do posts link to services, listings, products, locations, or consultations?
- Are calls to action clear?
- Does the blog help the business own important topics?
