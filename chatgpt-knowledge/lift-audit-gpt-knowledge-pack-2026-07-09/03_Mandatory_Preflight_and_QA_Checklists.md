# Mandatory Preflight and QA Checklists

## Before Research Ends

- Live website reviewed
- Services verified
- Locations verified
- Contact information verified
- Main audiences identified
- Strongest two opportunities selected
- Existing blog/social/listings reviewed where relevant

## Before Visual Production

- Exact deliverable confirmed
- One deliverable only
- Official logo visually inspected
- Current homepage or channel reviewed
- Official imagery sourced
- Brand colors confirmed
- Typography or visual style reviewed
- Business facts verified
- No generated people
- No invented claims
- No unsupported locations
- No fake statistics
- No duplicate asset use unless intentionally justified

## Single-Output Check

Before spending visual-generation tokens, ask:

- Is this one visual?
- Is this one channel?
- Does the title match the requested deliverable?
- Did another audit section accidentally enter the composition?
- Is this a website facelift, or did it drift into SEO?
- Is this a search graphic, or did it drift into a blog-page redesign?
- Is this written outreach, or did it drift into email marketing design?

## Final Visual QA

Reject outputs containing:

- wrong logo
- misspelled business name
- fake people
- fake reviews
- fake listings
- fake awards
- fake phone numbers
- duplicate images
- combined deliverables
- unreadable text
- distorted text
- layout collisions
- generic unrelated imagery
- unsupported claims
- inaccurate colors
- a design that no longer resembles the real brand

## Final Email QA

- Prospect-specific opening
- Recognizes what already works
- Explains the two selected opportunities
- Mentions attached visuals
- Includes Lift Studio paragraph
- Mentions attached service overview
- Uses a low-pressure CTA
- Ends at “Best, Megan”
- Gmail signature is not treated as email-body copy
