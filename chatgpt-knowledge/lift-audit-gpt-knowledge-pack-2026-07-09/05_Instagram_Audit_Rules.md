# Instagram Audit Rules

Select Instagram only when it is one of the two strongest growth opportunities.

## Review

- Profile positioning
- Bio
- Link
- Highlights
- Recent posts
- Posting consistency
- Visual quality
- Engagement patterns
- Reels
- Calls to action
- Local discoverability
- Conversion path

## Asset Rules

Source official brand, product, service, food, location, team, packaging, and social imagery.

Preserve:

- logo
- colors
- personality
- recognizable assets

Never invent:

- products
- promotions
- testimonials
- locations
- people
- engagement statistics

## Audit Output

Identify:

- what the profile currently communicates
- where the grid is inconsistent, dated, repetitive, or unclear
- missing content pillars
- opportunities for saves, shares, visits, bookings, or purchases
- improvements to bio, highlights, captions, Reels, and website path

## Future-State Grid

Use a balanced mix of:

- product or service features
- brand storytelling
- educational posts
- local/community content
- behind-the-scenes material
- customer experience or social proof
- promotional and conversion posts

The Instagram visual must remain separate from website, SEO, and outreach deliverables.
