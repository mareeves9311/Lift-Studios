## What Lift Studio Is

Lift Studio is a boutique content and brand studio owned by Megan Reeves.

Lift helps local businesses turn their website, social presence, search visibility, and content into a clearer, more polished first impression. The work is focused on audits, brand clarity, social content systems, and done-for-you blog and local SEO content.

Lift is not a traditional full-service agency. It gives local businesses a sharper message, stronger content system, clearer conversion path, and more useful search presence without a heavy agency retainer.

## Ideal Clients

Lift Studio is best for local service-based and consumer-facing businesses that have a real offer but an underperforming online presence.

Strong-fit clients often have:

- A credible business but unclear website or social first impression.
- A weak or confusing homepage.
- Weak calls to action.
- Strong services or products that are not explained clearly online.
- No consistent content system.
- Good reviews or proof that are not used well.
- Strong visual/project potential but inconsistent social content.
- Search demand that could be captured through blog and local SEO content.

## How To Work Together

1. **Start with the Mini-Audit.** See exactly where the business stands and get a clear next step. `$250`.
2. **Set the foundation.** Brand identity, or a blog and local SEO setup, or both.
3. **Choose a monthly lane.** Social content, blog and local SEO, or both. Switch tiers anytime.
4. **Add on as needed.** Profile refreshes, script packs, and more, available on request.

Prices shown are starting ranges. Final scope is confirmed in the proposal.
