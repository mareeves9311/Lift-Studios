## 2. Brand fonts

Both are available in Canva (Google Fonts). Set them as brand fonts:

- **Headings / Display:** Newsreader — weight **Light (300)**. This is the whole premium feel. Do not default to a bold sans.
- **Body / Subheads / UI:** Hanken Grotesk — Regular (400) and Bold (700).

Type feel to replicate from the site:
- Big display headlines, Newsreader Light, tight letter spacing (nudge tracking slightly negative).
- Short declarative body in Hanken Grotesk, generous line height.

---

## 3. Spacing rhythm

Keep layouts airy. Snap to this scale (px): 4, 8, 12, 16, 22, 32, 44, 64, 84. Default to more whitespace than feels necessary. Crowding is what makes a deck read as "agency," not "studio."

---

## 4. The rule that keeps it elevated

Cream background + one forest accent + Newsreader Light headline + lots of space. When in doubt, remove an element rather than add one.
