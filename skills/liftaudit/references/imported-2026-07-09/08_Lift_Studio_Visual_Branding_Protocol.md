# Lift Studio Branding on Audit Visuals

Every client-facing Lift Audit visual must include subtle, consistent Lift Studio branding.

## Purpose

Lift Studio branding should:

- Establish ownership of the audit concept
- Make shared or forwarded visuals traceable to Lift Studio
- Create consistency across all prospect audits
- Reinforce Lift Studio as the strategic source of the work

## Required Treatment

Use one of the following:

- Approved Lift Studio logo
- Approved Lift Studio wordmark
- “Prepared by Lift Studio”
- “A Lift Studio Audit”

Preferred placement:

- Bottom-right corner
- Bottom footer
- Small top-corner credit
- Discreet cover or title-area credit

## Hierarchy Rules

The prospect’s brand must remain primary.

Lift Studio branding must:

- Be smaller than the prospect logo
- Avoid competing with the main headline
- Avoid appearing inside the prospect’s simulated website navigation or header
- Never imply that Lift Studio is part of the prospect’s company
- Never replace or modify the prospect’s logo
- Remain visually secondary and unobtrusive

## Visual Consistency

Across audits, use a consistent:

- Lift Studio logo version
- Placement system
- Minimum size
- Margin and spacing
- Neutral or approved brand treatment

When possible, use a simple footer such as:

“Prepared by Lift Studio”

or

“Lift Studio | Brand, Content & Digital Growth”

## Channel Rules

Apply Lift Studio branding to:

- Website facelift concepts
- Search-to-Sale graphics
- Search-to-Service graphics
- Search-to-Deal graphics
- Search-to-Booking graphics
- Instagram audit concepts
- Blog and SEO opportunity visuals
- Stand-alone audit summary graphics

Do not insert Lift Studio branding into:

- The simulated prospect website header
- A fake prospect social profile bio
- A prospect logo lockup
- Any location that makes it appear to be part of the prospect’s official brand

## Preflight Check

Before finalizing a visual, confirm:

- Lift Studio branding is present
- It is legible
- It is visually secondary
- It does not compete with the prospect
- It does not look like part of the prospect’s identity
- It is placed consistently with other Lift Studio audit visuals
