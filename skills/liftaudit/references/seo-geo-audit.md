# SEO/GEO Audit

## Determine First

Does a real active blog, resource center, FAQ hub, or content hub exist?

If yes, audit cadence, recency, topic mix, intent, keyword targeting, internal linking, CTAs, local SEO, service/product connections, article structure, AI-search/GEO readiness, refresh opportunities, and thin/duplicate/outdated content.

If no, state that this is a content-launch opportunity. Do not fabricate sample existing posts.

## Framework

Search question -> useful answer -> relevant service/product -> proof and trust -> inquiry, booking, call, purchase, or visit.

## Deliverables

- Existing-blog audit or content-launch opportunity
- Search-intent analysis
- High-priority keyword and question themes
- Service/product page recommendations
- Local SEO recommendations
- GEO and AI-search recommendations
- Internal linking strategy
- Calls-to-action and conversion paths
- Content cadence
- Content refresh plan where applicable
- Topic ideas tied to business outcomes

Describe SEO/GEO as specialized local search and AI-search strategy, not generic blogging. Avoid ranking guarantees.
