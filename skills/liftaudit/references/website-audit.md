# Website Audit

## Focus

Prioritize hierarchy, navigation, conversion, trust, local search structure, and lead generation. Use real business information only.

## Inspect

- Above-the-fold clarity
- Header and navigation
- Primary CTA
- Service/product hierarchy
- Contact/booking/request flow
- Service-area and location cues
- Reviews, testimonials, certifications, guarantees, process, FAQs, and proof
- Internal links to key pages
- Technical or content inconsistencies visible to a prospect
- Current logo, colors, typography, imagery, icons, and layout

## Website Deliverables

- Full written website audit
- Website concept brief
- Editable HTML/CSS homepage concept
- Desktop screenshot
- Mobile screenshot when practical
- Source asset list
- Notes identifying conceptual imagery

## Concept Rules

Use the real logo, verified colors/type direction, official imagery when possible, recognizable brand elements, and real business information. Avoid invented statistics, reviews, awards, addresses, services, locations, uniforms, vehicles, or offers. Clearly label the concept as a concept, not a live rebuild.
