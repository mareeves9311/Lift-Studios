# Audit Protocol

## Required Sequence

1. Verify the live presence.
2. Write a factual audit before design.
3. Build the asset pack and brand snapshot.
4. Compare Website, Instagram, and Blog/SEO/GEO.
5. Select the two strongest channels unless Megan requests all three.
6. Create standalone deliverables only for selected channels.
7. Draft outreach after deliverables.
8. Run with what Codex can reliably access; flag anything better suited for ChatGPT, browser visual review, or Megan-supplied screenshots.

## Website Review

Inspect homepage, desktop/mobile presentation, header, navigation, footer, primary services/products, high-value service pages, about/team pages, contact/booking paths, locations/service areas, reviews/trust signals, offers, financing, memberships, blog/resources/FAQs, local-search structure, conversion paths, visible technical/content inconsistencies, and current visual system.

## Instagram Review

Inspect the public current profile: exact username, display name, bio, link, visible counts, profile image, highlights, grid, Reels, cadence, content categories, consistency, process/BTS content, local discoverability, CTAs, and conversion path. Do not invent missing profile data.

If Instagram blocks access or does not expose enough detail, mark the missing pieces as `Needs ChatGPT or Megan review` and ask for screenshots or a ChatGPT/browser pass instead of filling gaps.

## Blog/SEO/GEO Review

First determine whether a real active blog or content hub exists. If it exists, audit cadence, recency, topic mix, intent, keyword targeting, internal links, CTAs, local SEO, service/product connections, structure, AI-search/GEO readiness, refresh opportunities, and thin/duplicate/outdated content. If it does not exist, call it a content-launch opportunity, not an existing-blog audit.

## ChatGPT / Manual Review Flag

Use this flag whenever accuracy depends on a surface Codex cannot reliably inspect:

```text
Needs ChatGPT or Megan review:
- Surface:
- What Codex could verify:
- What still needs visual/browser review:
- Suggested thing to bring into ChatGPT:
```

Keep working on the remaining verified audit pieces.

## Required Written Output Before Design

Include overall verdict, what is working, primary weaknesses, conversion issues, content issues, trust issues, SEO/GEO opportunities, highest-value business opportunities, recommended channel priorities, and why the selected two channels have the strongest outreach value.

## Channel Selection

Select based on visible weakness, business upside, search intent, lead-generation value, and outreach usefulness. Document why the third channel was deprioritized.
