# Home Services SEO

Use this for home services, trades, automotive services, commercial services, attorneys, med spas, aesthetics, plastic surgery, chiropractors, and physical therapists when search demand and lead generation matter.

## Prioritize

- "Near me" searches
- Emergency searches
- Symptom and problem searches
- Repair-versus-replacement searches
- Cost and timeline questions
- Service-area pages
- Reviews and local proof
- Calls and request-service conversion
- High-value jobs
- Seasonal demand
- Commercial opportunities

## High-ROI Categories

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, landscaping, tree services, auto repair, septic, pest control, concrete, excavation, commercial cleaning, garage doors, pressure washing, fencing, deck/patio builders, dumpster rental, pool services, attorneys, med spas, aesthetics, plastic surgery, chiropractors, and physical therapists.

Do not invent job values or promise specific revenue. Explain ROI using reasonable business logic and label estimates clearly when supported.
